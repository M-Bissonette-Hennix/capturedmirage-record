# RECORD / CAPTUREDMIRAGE Branding

## Product relationship

**RECORD** is a distinct application inside the CAPTUREDMIRAGE ecosystem. RECORD acquires and certifies physical-game evidence; CAPTUREDMIRAGE performs downstream analysis.

Do not rebrand RECORD as CAPTUREDMIRAGE and do not present the CAPTUREDMIRAGE mark as RECORD's own app icon.

## Canonical CAPTUREDMIRAGE logo

Canonical asset:

`assets/branding/capturedmirage-logo.png`

SHA-256:

`df4f56b3ca47981e0202e433fe275bf15328ca58177599b3ba62a99cd796c8e6`

Dimensions: **1254 × 1254**.

The source image is the authority. It depicts a **pear-green combined chess figure: a knight in the foreground with a rook form behind/above it, on a pure-black background**. The pear-green artwork contains antialiasing/gradient variation; it must not be flattened to white, described as black-and-white, automatically recolored, or regenerated from an approximate vector unless a future brand revision explicitly replaces this asset.

The CAPTUREDMIRAGE interface accent remains `#c6de5b`. The source logo's foreground is visually in that pear-green family but the raster itself contains tonal variation and should be used directly.
